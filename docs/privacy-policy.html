Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MarkHola macOS application handles information.

Information MarkHola handles

MarkHola is a local Markdown reader, editor, and exporter. Documents that you open, edit, save, print, or export are processed on your Mac. MarkHola does not require an account and does not send the contents of your documents to MarkHola.

Information we collect

MarkHola does not itself collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or document content. MarkHola does not include advertising or a third-party analytics SDK.

Links and third-party services

MarkHola may display links included in Markdown documents or in app information. When you choose to open an external link, it is handled by your default browser and the destination's privacy policy applies. MarkHola does not control information collected by those destinations.

App Store and operating-system services

Apple may process information needed to distribute, update, secure, and operate apps through the App Store and macOS. Those activities are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy, not this policy.

Children's privacy

MarkHola does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MarkHola's information practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the MarkHola GitHub repository.
